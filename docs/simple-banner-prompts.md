# Simple facade artwork

Mode: built-in imagegen, generation. Optimized files: public/images/district/simple. Original outputs: docs/artwork-simple-originals. Five separate generations; artwork is designed for legibility at 220px wide.

## safehouse

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one landscape 16:9 minimalist editorial illustration for a small roadside banner in a GTA San Andreas-inspired portfolio. It must remain instantly legible when reduced to 220 pixels wide. One very large central subject fills 70% of the image, bold confident ink contours, only 3 or 4 broad flat color shapes with subtle painted shading, generous uncluttered negative space. Sophisticated 2000s game loading-screen aesthetic. Cohesive palette: cream #eee0bf, muted sunset amber #dca24e, dark olive #20372a and charcoal. Strong light/dark separation, luminous cream backdrop with a simple amber sun disk, no scene packed with objects, no tiny architecture, no fine texture, no text, no letters, no logos, no border, no UI. ABOUT / THE SAFEHOUSE: one large inviting dark green arched doorway in a simple cream stucco wall, door partly open to a broad warm amber glow. Just one simple palm-leaf shadow at the far edge. A bold welcoming home/origin-story symbol; flat graphic illustration with only the doorway, wall and light.
